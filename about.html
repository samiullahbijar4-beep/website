OUR PURPOSE

Language brings us closer.

panjlingu is a simple digital dictionary designed to make word discovery and cross-language learning easier.

What you can do

Search words, explore translations in five languages, and share suggestions when you notice a missing word or an improvement.

Our language collection

The dictionary supports English, Urdu, Sindhi, Arabic, and Spanish by default. An administrator can update the language list from the admin panel.

Help us grow

Dictionary quality improves with thoughtful feedback. Visit the feedback page to send a suggestion or correction.
